# The Hard Questions

> The core of this project. Before any venture earns time or money, it must survive these questions. Answer them honestly — "I don't know yet" is a valid answer that becomes a research task. Copy this list into a venture's folder and fill it in.

Use with: [venture lifecycle](venture-lifecycle.md) · [evaluation rubric](evaluation-rubric.md) · Romania facts in [`../knowledge/`](../knowledge/).

---

## 1. Problem & need
- What specific problem does this solve, and for whom (be concrete)?
- How acute is the pain? What do people do today instead?
- What's the evidence the problem is real — not just assumed? (data, interviews, your own experience)
- Why now? What changed (tech, law, market, funding) that makes this timely?
- If this venture didn't exist, who would notice?

## 2. Market & demand (Romania)
- Who is the customer/beneficiary, and who actually pays?
- TAM / SAM / SOM in Romania — order-of-magnitude is fine, but show the math.
- Is demand local (RO), regional (CEE/EU), or export?
- Who are the competitors / substitutes? Why would anyone switch?
- What's the willingness to pay, and what's the price anchor today?

## 3. Legal structure
- Which legal form fits — SRL, SRL-D, SA, PFA/II, or an NGO (asociație/fundație)? Why? (see [`../knowledge/company-formation.md`](../knowledge/company-formation.md))
- For a nonprofit: does the mission require an asociație/fundație rather than a company?
- Ownership/shareholding split? Founders' agreement? Vesting?
- Where is the registered seat (*sediu social*), and do you have rights to that address?
- Which CAEN Rev. 3 activity codes (primary + secondary)?

## 4. Regulation, permits & licensing
- What authorizations does this activity need, and from which authority? (e.g. **ANRE** for energy, **APIA/AFIR** + **ANSVSA** for agri/food, **ANSPDCP** for personal data) — see [`../knowledge/institutions.md`](../knowledge/institutions.md)
- Does it process personal data? **Children's data?** → GDPR + ANSPDCP obligations (see [`../knowledge/regulatory-compliance.md`](../knowledge/regulatory-compliance.md))
- Sector-specific norms, standards, certifications (CE, ISO, environmental permits, food safety)?
- e-Factura / SAF-T / e-Transport obligations from day one?
- Any state-aid / competition constraints if taking public funding?

## 5. Capital & funding
- How much to reach the first real milestone? How much to break-even?
- Funding mix: own equity, debt, EU funds, national grants, revenue? (see [`../knowledge/funding-landscape.md`](../knowledge/funding-landscape.md))
- Are you eligible for the grant you're counting on — and what's the deadline & co-financing %? (⚠️ PNRR closes Aug 2026; Cohesion 2021–27 is the live channel)
- What's the cash runway, and what's the burn rate?
- What does the money buy that creates a defensible asset?

## 6. Financial viability
- Unit economics: revenue per unit/customer minus the cost to serve. Positive?
- Fixed vs variable costs; gross margin; path to break-even.
- Which tax regime — micro (1% on revenue, ≤ €100k) or CIT (16%)? At what point do you cross over? (see [`../knowledge/taxation.md`](../knowledge/taxation.md))
- Payroll reality: gross → total employer cost (≈ +2.25% CAM) and gross → net (CAS 25% + CASS 10% + 10% income tax).
- VAT (21%) — registered or not? Impact on pricing and cash flow.
- Sensitivity: what kills the model (price drop, cost spike, slow ramp)?

## 7. Team & operations
- Who runs it day-to-day? What's the skills gap?
- Build vs hire vs outsource for the core capability?
- Key suppliers/partners — and your dependency / lock-in risk.
- What does "operations" actually look like in week 1, month 6, year 2?

## 8. Location & strategic fit (Romania)
- Why this location in Romania? (grid capacity & permits for energy; water/soil/logistics for agri; talent pools for software)
- Infrastructure dependencies: power, grid connection, water, transport, broadband.
- Proximity to customers, inputs, talent, or funding-eligibility zones (less-developed regions get higher EU co-financing).
- Regional development incentives or county-level support?

## 9. Risks & mitigations
- Top 5 risks across: regulatory, market, execution, financing, technology, political.
- For each: likelihood × impact, an early-warning signal, and a mitigation.
- What's the single biggest assumption that, if wrong, sinks the venture?
- Kill criteria: what evidence would make you stop?

## 10. Impact & theory of change (mission-driven ventures)
- For nonprofit / education / social ventures: who benefits, and how does the activity *cause* that benefit?
- What's measured, and what does success look like in numbers?
- **Safeguarding:** for anything involving children, what protects them (data, content, contact)?
- Sustainability: how does impact continue without you / without one-off grants?

## 11. Timeline & milestones
- The 5–7 milestones from today to "real": each with a date and a definition of done.
- Hard external deadlines (funding calls, grant windows, regulatory cutoffs).
- Critical path & dependencies — what blocks what?
- What's the smallest version you can put in front of a real user/customer, and when?

---

### How to use the answers
- Gaps become research tasks (log them in the venture's [decision log](../templates/decision-log.md)).
- The honest answers feed the [evaluation rubric](evaluation-rubric.md) to compare ventures objectively.
- Revisit at each [lifecycle](venture-lifecycle.md) gate — answers change as you learn.
