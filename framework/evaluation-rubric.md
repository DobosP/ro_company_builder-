# Venture Evaluation Rubric

A simple, repeatable way to compare ventures and decide where to spend time and money. Score each dimension **1 (weak) – 5 (strong)**, multiply by the weight, and sum. Re-score at each [lifecycle](venture-lifecycle.md) gate as evidence improves.

| # | Dimension | What a 5 looks like | Weight |
|---|-----------|---------------------|:------:|
| 1 | **Problem intensity** | Acute, evidenced pain; clear "why now" | 3 |
| 2 | **Market size & demand (RO/EU)** | Large, reachable, willing to pay | 3 |
| 3 | **Financial viability** | Positive unit economics, clear path to break-even | 3 |
| 4 | **Fundability** | Strong fit to a live grant/loan, or self-funding | 2 |
| 5 | **Regulatory feasibility** | Clear, surmountable permit path | 2 |
| 6 | **Team / execution fit** | Founders can actually build & run it | 3 |
| 7 | **Defensibility / moat** | Hard to copy; durable advantage | 2 |
| 8 | **Impact** (mission ventures) | Clear theory of change, measurable benefit | 2 |
| 9 | **Strategic location fit (RO)** | Infrastructure / eligibility strongly favorable | 1 |
| 10 | **Risk profile** (inverted) | Few fatal risks; credible mitigations | 2 |

**Score = Σ (rating × weight).** Maximum = 115.

### Interpretation (guideline, not gospel)
- **> 85** — strong; commit resources.
- **60–85** — promising; close the biggest gaps first.
- **< 60** — reshape or shelve; identify the single blocker.

### Rules of thumb
- A **1 on any "fatal" dimension** (regulatory feasibility, financial viability) caps the venture regardless of total — fix it or stop.
- Weight **impact** higher for the nonprofit / education ventures; weight **financial viability** higher for the commercial ones (energy, agri).
- Record the score and the reasoning in the venture's [decision log](../templates/decision-log.md) so the call is auditable later.

> The rubric is a thinking aid, not an oracle. A low score is an invitation to reshape the venture, not always to kill it.
