# Venture Lifecycle (Romania)

Stages every venture passes through, with the Romania-specific gate at each one. Don't advance until the gate's questions (from [hard-questions.md](hard-questions.md)) are answered.

| Stage | Goal | Romania-specific gate | Key docs |
|-------|------|------------------------|----------|
| **0. Idea** | Capture the concept | Hard questions §1–2 answered at a basic level | [venture brief](../templates/venture-brief.md) |
| **1. Validate** | Prove the problem & demand are real | Evidence of demand; competitor scan; rough TAM | [market analysis](../templates/market-analysis.md) |
| **2. Model** | Show it can make money / sustain | Tax regime chosen (micro vs CIT); unit economics positive; funding path identified | [financial model](../templates/financial-model.md), [funding](../knowledge/funding-landscape.md) |
| **3. Form** | Become a legal entity | Legal form + CAEN codes chosen; ONRC registration; **CUI + J-number** issued | [formation](../knowledge/company-formation.md) |
| **4. Comply** | Be operable & legal | Fiscal registration (ANAF), e-Factura/SAF-T set up, sector permits, GDPR | [compliance](../knowledge/regulatory-compliance.md) |
| **5. Operate** | Deliver to first customers | First revenue / first beneficiaries; bookkeeping running | [business plan](../templates/business-plan.md) |
| **6. Fund/Scale** | Grow with capital | Grant/loan secured or revenue-funded growth; hiring within payroll model | [funding](../knowledge/funding-landscape.md) |
| **7. Sustain/Exit** | Endure or transition | Profitability, endowment (NGO), acquisition, or wind-down plan | [risk register](../templates/risk-register.md) |

## Notes

- **Stages 3–4 are uniquely Romanian** and where most founders underestimate effort: ONRC filing, ANAF fiscal registration, and the mandatory e-invoicing/reporting stack (e-Factura, SAF-T D406, e-TVA).
- **Funding deadlines can reorder the stages.** A grant window (e.g. a Cohesion 2021–27 call) may force you to form the entity earlier than demand alone would justify. Track these in §11 of the hard questions.
- **NGOs (asociație/fundație) follow a parallel track:** registration is through the court (Registrul Asociațiilor și Fundațiilor), not ONRC — note this when the venture is mission-first (e.g. the children's nonprofit app).
- **Re-score at each gate** with the [evaluation rubric](evaluation-rubric.md); a venture can fail a later gate even after passing earlier ones.
