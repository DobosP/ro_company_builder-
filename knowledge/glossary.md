# Glossary — Romanian Terms & Acronyms

> Decodes the Romanian terms used across this knowledge base. See also [institutions.md](institutions.md).

## Legal forms
- **SRL** — *Societate cu Răspundere Limitată*: limited-liability company (the default).
- **SRL-D** — SRL for first-time entrepreneurs (*debutant*), with incentives/conditions.
- **SA** — *Societate pe Acțiuni*: joint-stock company.
- **PFA** — *Persoană Fizică Autorizată*: authorized sole trader (personal liability).
- **II** — *Întreprindere Individuală*: individual enterprise.
- **Asociație / Fundație** — nonprofit association / foundation (OG 26/2000).

## Registration & identifiers
- **ONRC** — National Trade Registry Office.
- **CUI** — *Cod Unic de Înregistrare*: unique company registration code.
- **CIF** — fiscal identification code (often the same digits as CUI).
- **J-number** — Trade Registry entry number (e.g. *J40/.../2026*).
- **CAEN** — Romanian activity classification (Rev. 3 mandatory from 2026).
- **act constitutiv** — Articles of Association.
- **sediu social** — registered office / seat.

## Tax & reporting
- **TVA** — *Taxa pe Valoarea Adăugată*: VAT.
- **CIT** — corporate income tax (16%).
- **IMCA** — minimum turnover tax for very large companies.
- **CAS** — pension social-insurance contribution.
- **CASS** — health-insurance contribution.
- **CAM** — employer work-insurance contribution.
- **e-Factura** — mandatory e-invoicing system.
- **SAF-T / D406** — Standard Audit File for Tax declaration.
- **e-TVA** — pre-filled VAT return.
- **e-Transport** — goods-movement reporting system.
- **REGES** (Revisal) — employee register.
- **ANAF** — National tax-administration agency.

## Funding & institutions
- **PNRR** — *Planul Național de Redresare și Reziliență*: National Recovery & Resilience Plan.
- **MIPE** — Ministry of European Investments and Projects.
- **ERDF / ESF+** — European Regional Development Fund / European Social Fund Plus.
- **de minimis** — small state-aid scheme under EU thresholds.
- **ANRE / APIA / AFIR / ANSVSA / ANSPDCP / ANCOM** — sector authorities (see [institutions.md](institutions.md)).
