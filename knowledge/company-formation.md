# Company Formation in Romania

> ⚠️ **Not legal/tax advice. Verified 2026-05; Romanian law changes frequently — re-verify with ONRC/a lawyer before acting.** Sources: [`sources.md`](sources.md).

## Legal forms at a glance

| Form | Romanian | Best for | Liability | Min. capital (2026) |
|------|----------|----------|-----------|----------------------|
| **SRL** | Societate cu Răspundere Limitată | Most ventures (LLC); 1–50 associates | Limited | **RON 500 (~€100)** [[S1]](sources.md#s1) [[S5]](sources.md#s5) |
| **SRL-D** | SRL — Debutant | First-time entrepreneurs (incentives + conditions) | Limited | as SRL |
| **SA** | Societate pe Acțiuni | Larger/regulated, share issuance; ≥2 shareholders | Limited | **RON 90,000** (~€18k; unchanged for 2026) [[S24]](sources.md#s24) |
| **PFA** | Persoană Fizică Autorizată | Solo professional / freelancer | **Personal** | — |
| **II** | Întreprindere Individuală | Individual enterprise | **Personal** | — |
| **Asociație / Fundație** | — | Nonprofit / mission-first | n/a | OG 26/2000; registered via **court**, not ONRC |

The **SRL** is the default for almost all commercial ventures. Use an **NGO** form when the mission is non-commercial (relevant to the planned children's nonprofit app).

## SRL share capital (2026 change)
- Minimum **RON 500 (~€100)**, up from the former symbolic 1 RON. [[S1]](sources.md#s1) [[S5]](sources.md#s5)
- Must increase to **RON 5,000** if annual turnover exceeds **~RON 400,000 (~€80k)**; companies that cross the threshold comply by the end of the following financial year. [[S3]](sources.md#s3) [[S4]](sources.md#s4)

**SA (joint-stock):** minimum **RON 90,000** — unchanged for 2026 (regulated periodically to track ~€25k). ≥2 shareholders; **≥30%** of capital paid at incorporation (balance within 12 months); minimum nominal value RON 0.10/share. [[S24]](sources.md#s24)

## Registration steps (ONRC)
1. **Reserve the company name** at ONRC (Trade Registry). [[S1]](sources.md#s1)
2. **Draft the Articles of Association** (*act constitutiv*) — share capital, associates' rights, management, profit split, and **CAEN Rev. 3** activity codes (**mandatory since 1 Jan 2026**). [[S1]](sources.md#s1)
3. **Deposit share capital** — bank deposit certificate or a subscription declaration. [[S1]](sources.md#s1)
4. **File with ONRC** — registration request + the fiscal-registration declaration (for ANAF). [[S1]](sources.md#s1)
5. **Receive the Registration Certificate** with the **CUI** (unique registration code) and **J-number** — typically **3–5 business days**. [[S1]](sources.md#s1)

- **Costs:** ~**RON 200–300** in government/publication fees. [[S1]](sources.md#s1)
- **Remote filing** is possible via the ONRC online portal. [[S1]](sources.md#s1) [[S2]](sources.md#s2)

## CAEN Rev. 3 codes
- Every activity is classified by a **CAEN** code; **Rev. 3** is mandatory in Articles of Association from 1 Jan 2026. [[S1]](sources.md#s1)
- Choose one **primary** code and any **secondary** codes you may use. Some codes trigger sector authorizations (see [regulatory-compliance.md](regulatory-compliance.md) and [institutions.md](institutions.md)).
