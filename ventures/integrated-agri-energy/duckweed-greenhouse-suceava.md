# Year-Round Duckweed Greenhouse in Suceava — materials & cost

> *How expensive, and what materials, to grow duckweed **year-round for animal feed** in cold Suceava?* Verified 2026-05; [I#] → [sources](research/sources.md). Honest headline below.

## The honest headline
**Year-round is the expensive mode.** Duckweed needs **water at 20–25°C** and decent light; it stops growing <15°C and dies near freezing. [I18](research/sources.md#i18) In Suceava (−20 to −25°C, heavy snow, ~8 h weak winter sun) that means **heating water + adding light through Dec–Feb** — the cost driver. Two routes:

| Route | What | All-in cost | When it makes sense |
|-------|------|-------------|---------------------|
| **A. True year-round** (heated + lit) | keep water 20–25°C + LED light all winter | **~€400–600/m²** of water surface | you genuinely need *fresh* feed in winter |
| **B. Seasonal + store** ⭐ | grow ~8–9 months (Apr–Oct) cheaply, **dry/ensile surplus** for winter feed | **~€150–250/m²** | usually the smarter, cheaper choice for feed |

For **animal feed**, Route B is normally far better value — drying summer duckweed beats burning fuel to keep water tropical in January. Decide which you actually need before spending.

## Materials list (Route A — true year-round)
**1. Structure (cold + snow rated)**
- **Steel frame** + **twin- or triple-wall polycarbonate** glazing (double the insulation of single poly/glass). [I19](research/sources.md#i19)
- **Insulated solid north wall** (Chinese-solar-greenhouse style) — glazed south, insulated north → big winter heat saver.
- **Snow load ≥150–250 kg/m²** (Suceava is a heavy-snow zone — *[verify local sₖ value]*); steep roof pitch to shed snow.
- **Night thermal screen / blackout-insulation curtain** (cuts night heat loss sharply).

**2. Duckweed growing system**
- **Shallow basins / raceways, 15–20 cm deep** [I18](research/sources.md#i18) — concrete, food-grade tanks, or lined channels.
- **Pond liner** (EPDM/HDPE food-grade) if earthen/lined basins.
- **Low-speed circulation** (small pump or paddlewheel) + gentle **aeration**.
- **Harvesting gear** (skimmer net/mesh conveyor) + draining basket.

**3. Heating (the key cost — heat the WATER, not the air)**
- **Biomass (wood/pellet) boiler** + a hot-water loop with **pipes under/through the basins** + heat exchanger + thermostat. ⭐ **Cheapest for forested Suceava** — local wood is abundant and cheap. [I19](research/sources.md#i19)
- Alternative: **air/water-source heat pump** (efficient, but electricity cost; COP falls in deep cold).
- **Insulate under and around the basins** (the water mass then holds heat well).

**4. Power (solar + your second-hand batteries)**
- Small **solar array (~5–10 kWp)** + **second-hand batteries** + inverter/charge controller — run **pumps, aeration, controls, and LED lights**, **not** bulk heat (winter solar is too weak for that). Your free engineer builds this.

**5. Lighting (needed for true winter growth)**
- **LED grow lights** over the basins to extend the short Suceava winter photoperiod (duckweed needs light to keep growing).

**6. Controls & nutrients**
- Sensors/automation: **water temp, pH, level, air temp** (low-labour).
- **Nutrient dosing:** diluted **manure / compost tea** tank — duckweed needs N/P to grow fast (cheap; closes the loop with any livestock).

## Indicative cost — small starter (~150 m² greenhouse, ~100 m² water)
| Item | Route A (year-round) | Route B (seasonal+dry) |
|------|----------------------:|------------------------:|
| Structure (insulated twin-wall, snow-rated) | €30–45k | €22–35k |
| Basins/raceways + liner + plumbing | €6–12k | €6–12k |
| Circulation + aeration + harvest | €4–8k | €4–8k |
| **Heating** (biomass boiler + water loop + insulation) | €8–15k | €2–4k (frost only) |
| Solar + second-hand batteries + inverter | €8–15k | €5–9k |
| **LED grow lights** | €5–12k | €0–2k |
| Controls/sensors + nutrient dosing | €3–6k | €2–4k |
| Drying/storage (for winter feed) | — | €3–6k |
| **Total (~150 m²)** | **~€65–110k** | **~€45–75k** |
| **≈ per m² (water surface)** | **~€400–600/m²** | **~€150–250/m²** |

*(Land = €0, you own it. Engineer labour = €0, in-kind. Add ~10% contingency.)*

## What it produces (feed)
At ~**1–3 kg dry matter/m²/yr** [I18](research/sources.md#i18), ~100 m² of water gives **~100–300 kg DM/yr** — a **protein supplement** (~30–43% crude protein) for poultry/goats at ~20% of their ration, **not** a sole feed and not enough for cattle. [I16](research/sources.md#i16) Scale water area to the herd.

## Funding
**AFIR DR-14 (€75k, 85%)** can cover most of either route [I9](research/sources.md#i9) — but remember it **reimburses after you spend**, so budget bridge cash. See [phase1-small-start.md](phase1-small-start.md).

## Recommendation
- If feed is the goal: **start with Route B** (seasonal grow + dried winter store) — ~€45–75k for ~150 m², far cheaper, and a wood-fired loop gives cheap frost protection.
- Go **Route A** only for the part of the year/quantity you truly need fresh, or once the loop is proven.
- **Heat the water with biomass** (Suceava wood), keep **solar + second-hand batteries for pumps/lights/controls**, and **insulate hard** (twin-wall + north wall + night screen) — that's what makes a Suceava winter affordable.
