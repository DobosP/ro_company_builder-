## Why now
Every loop maps to a **live, generous fund**, and Romania explicitly enables **agrivoltaics + floating PV** (Law 254/2022) and **sustainable aquaculture** (EMFAF) — so a largely **grant-funded** circular system is achievable today. [I1](research/sources.md#i1) [I7](research/sources.md#i7)

## Biggest risk / open question
**Complexity + sequencing:** can it be **phased** so each module is funded, built and operating before the next — without over-abstracting groundwater or over-promising the "free fertiliser" loop? [I2](research/sources.md#i2) [I3](research/sources.md#i3)

## Next 3 actions
1. **Pick the lead module** (recommend Phase 1: solar + storage + solar pumps + first lake) and apply to its fund.
2. **Engage a grant consultant** to validate the **SPV + cumulation** structure. [funding-stack.md](funding-stack.md)
3. **Site + hydrogeology + Apele Române** abstraction check; energy/water balance for self-sufficiency. [A9](../agriculture-irrigation/research/sources.md#a9)
