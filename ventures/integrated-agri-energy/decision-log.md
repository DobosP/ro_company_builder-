## 2026-05-29 — Small-start path defined (own land, free engineer, low labour, ~€100k)
- **Scope:** small greenhouse + drip irrigation + **duckweed pond** (biofertiliser loop) + small solar + **second-hand batteries** (engineer builds free) + optional indoor **spirulina** for protein. High-margin crop = **herbs/microgreens/leafy greens**. [I11](research/sources.md#i11) [I15](research/sources.md#i15)
- **🎯 Grant fit:** **AFIR DR-14 small farms — €75,000, 85%**, sessions from 27 Feb 2026 — ideal for "start small, manageable solo." [I9](research/sources.md#i9)
- **finsim** (~€100k project, €75k grant, €30k cash, **micro 1%** regime): **IRR ~25%, payback 2032, break-even 2029** — viable. [finsim-phase1-small-report.md](finsim-phase1-small-report.md)
- **⚠️ Two real traps flagged:** (1) DR-14 **reimburses after spend** → must **bridge ~€50–55k**; (2) heating a greenhouse electrically in a RO winter is **15–30 kWh/day** → too much for a small battery, so **passive solar + thermal mass (water barrels + the pond) first**, electricity only for **frost protection / snow-melt**. [I13](research/sources.md#i13) [I14](research/sources.md#i14)
- **Protein plant:** spirulina (20× protein/ha vs soy) but needs warmth/pH → put it **in tanks inside the heated greenhouse**; **duckweed** is the hardy outdoor pond plant for fertiliser. [I10](research/sources.md#i10) [I11](research/sources.md#i11)
- **Decision:** start with this DR-14 small build; defer fish (EMFAF) and the rest to later phases. Full plan: [phase1-small-start.md](phase1-small-start.md).

## 2026-05-29 — finsim proves it: phase it (self-consumption base first), don't build all at once
- **All-at-once** (solar+storage+pumps+lakes+greenhouse+aquaculture, €5M CAPEX, €2.23M stacked grants): **IRR 0.5%, NPV −€1.75M, financing gap −€1.17M** → unviable as a single build. [finsim-report.md](finsim-report.md)
- **Phase 1 only** (solar+storage+pumps+greenhouse, €3.4M CAPEX, €1.4M grants, €1.5M equity): **IRR 10.0%, NPV +€6k, no gap, payback 2034** → fundable. [finsim-phase1-report.md](finsim-phase1-report.md)
- **Lesson:** the self-consumption energy+water+greenhouse base works; **aquaculture/algae must be a later, separately-funded (EMFAF) phase**, not bolted on up front. DSCR is tight in the greenhouse ramp → seek a **debt grace period**.
- **Decision:** adopt the **4-phase plan** (energy/water → greenhouse → fish/algae → processing/circular); structure as **HoldCo + SPVs** to stack funds without double-funding. See [funding-stack.md](funding-stack.md), [system-design](research/system-design.md).
