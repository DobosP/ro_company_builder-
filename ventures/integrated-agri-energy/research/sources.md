<a id="i1"></a>**I1.** European Commission — **EMFAF** (European Maritime, Fisheries & Aquaculture Fund) 2021–27, **>€6B**; supports **sustainable aquaculture**; **max co-financing 70%**; applied via the national managing authority — https://oceans-and-fisheries.ec.europa.eu/funding-and-investment/emfaf_en — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i2"></a>**I2.** ScienceDirect / OSU Extension — **aquaponics economics:** large capital + opex often >$100k; revenue **dominated by plant sales**; ~100 m² runs at a loss, **>1,000 m² needed for profitability**; takes ~2 yrs to profit — https://extension.okstate.edu/fact-sheets/economics-of-aquaponics.html — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i3"></a>**I3.** ScienceDirect — aquaponics nutrient loop: fish manure/algae/decomposing feed = liquid fertiliser, **but fish feed is 7–14× (N) / 17–88× (P) more expensive** than inorganic fertiliser per nutrient → natural-fertiliser value is real but not free — https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0144860921000613 — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i4"></a>**I4.** CF Greenhouse / topgreenhousetech — **smart greenhouse climate-control + sensors ≈ $70–120/m²** → **~€0.7–1.2M/ha**; automation cuts labour — https://www.cfgreenhouse.com/news/how-much-does-it-really-cost-to-build-a-smart-greenhouse-in-2025/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i5"></a>**I5.** All About Feed / Mordor — **algae** production cost **~€1.4–2.25/kg** (flat-panel scaled) vs ~€6/kg raceway; biofertiliser + feed + protein uses; EU added 20 algae species to Novel Food catalogue (2024) — https://www.allaboutfeed.net/animal-feed/raw-materials/algae-cultivation-profitable-by-2025/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i6"></a>**I6.** Morca / VEICHI / DIFFUL — **solar borehole pumps:** submersible to ~250 m depth, **MPPT controllers >99% efficient**; size Max Head 450–500 ft for water-table drop; cuts diesel/grid cost — https://morcapumps.com/blog/solar-pump-for-deep-borewells/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i7"></a>**I7.** CMS / Law 254/2022 — **Romania agrivoltaics:** renewable generation allowed on **extra-muros Class III/IV/V** ag land up to 50 ha; **floating PV** active (Renera 50 MW Brăila; Gălățui 259 MW incl. 50 MW storage) → solar + water + storage co-location is permitted & happening — https://cms.law/en/int/expert-guides/cms-expert-guide-to-agrivoltaics-and-floating-photovoltaics/romania — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i8"></a>**I8.** AgriSolar Clearinghouse — **aquavoltaics** (solar over/around aquaculture ponds): shades water (less evaporation/algae bloom), generates power, dual land use — https://www.agrisolarclearinghouse.org/aquavoltaics/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i9"></a>**I9.** AFIR / StartupCafe / agrointel — **DR-14 "Investments in small farms":** up to **€75,000/project** (raised from €50k), **85% intensity**, €108M budget, sessions from **27 Feb 2026** — the small-scale grant — https://startupcafe.ro/fonduri-ue-2026-granturi-50000-euro-pfa-srl-ferme-mici-ghid-dr-14-de-afir-98500 — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i10"></a>**I10.** SpirulinaFarming / Springer — **spirulina:** up to **20× more protein/ha than soy**, low water; manual farms rarely profit, automation/certification needed; nutrients & pH/temperature control required — https://spirulinafarming.com/blog/is-spirulina-farming-profitable-in-2025-real-costs-roi-market-forecast/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i11"></a>**I11.** MDPI / ScienceDirect — **duckweed (Lemna minor):** hardy pond plant, ~27–40% protein, yields up to **28.5 g/m²/day**; grows on diluted effluent → ideal cheap **biofertiliser + feed** loop — https://www.mdpi.com/2223-7747/11/8/1010 — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i12"></a>**I12.** PMC / ScienceDirect — **algae/cyanobacteria biofertiliser:** N-fixing species (Anabaena, Nostoc, Spirulina, Chlorella) replace some N/P, add growth hormones, improve soil & water retention — apply harvested algae to crops — https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8961072/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i13"></a>**I13.** Greenhouse Score / Mother Earth News — **passive-solar cold-climate greenhouse:** thermal mass (water barrels, dark, in sun path) + insulated north wall keeps **+15–20°F overnight**; passive alone insufficient in snow → small active backup for frost — https://www.greenhousescore.blog/blog/cold-climate-winter-greenhouses/passive-solar-greenhouse-climate-proof-year-round/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i14"></a>**I14.** EcoFlow / Hartley — **greenhouse winter heating energy:** small (<18 m²) ~5–10 kWh/day, mid (18–55 m²) ~15–30 kWh/day; 2–3 days autonomy needs 30–80 kWh battery → electric-only winter heat is very battery-heavy — https://www.ecoflow.com/us/blog/greenhouse-battery-heating-guide — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i15"></a>**I15.** Market Gardener / CF Greenhouse — **highest-margin greenhouse crops:** microgreens, specialty herbs (Thai/purple basil), leafy greens (fast cycles), tomatoes (up to 55 kg/m²), edible flowers, mushrooms — best €/m² for small growers — https://themarketgardener.com/farming-techniques/the-15-most-profitable-crops-for-small-farms/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i16"></a>**I16.** All About Feed / Feedipedia — **duckweed as livestock feed:** 10–30 t DM/ha/yr, **up to 43% crude protein**, sits **between grass silage and soybean meal**; fed to cattle/sheep/goats **only as part of the diet** (~20% optimum; full substitution lowers performance) — https://www.allaboutfeed.net/all-about/new-proteins/duckweed-for-sheep-goats-and-cattle/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="i17"></a>**I17.** Greenhouse Grower / Mother Earth — **ginger & turmeric in heated greenhouse:** optimum **22–25°C + constant humidity**, container-grown, go dormant when cold — high-value "superfood" spices well-suited to a warm-humid greenhouse (the duckweed-pond microclimate) — https://www.greenhousegrower.com/production/how-to-grow-superfoods-ginger-and-turmeric-in-containers/ — *verified 2026-05*
