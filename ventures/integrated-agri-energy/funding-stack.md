# Funding Stack — one system, many funds

> The strategic core of this venture: **structure the integrated system as a holding company + per-module SPVs**, each SPV drawing its **own** EU/RO funding stream. Legitimate because each is a distinct asset with distinct objectives — **provided no single eligible cost is funded twice** (state-aid cumulation). Verified 2026-05; cross-refs to the [funding playbook](../../framework/eu-funding-playbook.md).

## Why this is powerful
- A **€5–10M** integrated system could need only **~€2–4M** own equity/debt after stacking 4–6 grants at 50–80% each.
- Each fund **de-risks a different layer** — the system isn't betting on one programme.
- **Self-consumption** means inter-module "sales" (power, water, fertiliser) are internal cost savings, improving each SPV's standalone case.

## Rules & risks (must respect)
- **No double funding:** one euro of cost → one fund only. Allocate costs cleanly per SPV. (CISAF/GBER/de-minimis cumulation caps.)
- **Different call windows:** AFIR (Dec–Feb), EMFAF, Modernisation Fund (Q2 2026) open at different times → **sequence applications**; phase the build accordingly.
- **Admin load:** 6 SPVs × 6 grant processes = heavy reporting/audit → budget for a **grant manager / consultant**.
- **Inter-company transactions** (power/water/fertiliser between SPVs) need arm's-length pricing for tax + state-aid cleanliness.
