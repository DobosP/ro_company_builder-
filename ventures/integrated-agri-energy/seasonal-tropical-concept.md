# Seasonal Warm-Months Concept (Suceava) — cheap, low-effort, tropical

> Your refined plan: **Suceava, warm months only** (skip costly winter heating), a **self-sustaining low-effort pond**, and a **tropical high-margin** crop setup. Verified 2026-05; [I#] → [sources](research/sources.md).

## The season (Suceava)
- **Warm/usable: ~May–September**; frost-free roughly **late April–early October** → **~5–6 months**. July avg high **~25°C**, with heatwaves higher (your "really warm summer"). [I23](research/sources.md#i23)
- So in the warm months it's **genuinely warm enough for tropicals with no heating** — you only need **frost protection** in the shoulder weeks and **cooling** at peak summer.

## The cheap concept (vs year-round)
- **Drop the biomass boiler / winter water-heating + winter LEDs** — that was the expensive part. Run on **passive solar + ventilation**, with a tiny frost heater only for spring/autumn nights.
- **Winter = dormant:** harvest & **store ginger/turmeric** (they're harvested in autumn anyway), **dry surplus duckweed** for winter animal feed, leave the greenhouse cold. Near-zero winter cost.
- **Cost falls** from ~€400–600/m² (year-round) toward **~€120–200/m²** — no boiler, no winter lighting; see [duckweed-greenhouse-suceava.md](duckweed-greenhouse-suceava.md) Route B.

## Self-sustaining, low-effort pond — what to add
The goal: balance **nutrients in = harvest out**, let cover do the pest control, and automate circulation — so the only routine task is **harvesting** (which is the yield).

| Add this | Why | Effort |
|----------|-----|--------|
| **Native duckweed (Lemna minor)** | the crop; native = **legal** ✓ | harvest only |
| **Small manure / compost-tea drip** (a few chickens/rabbits) | passive **N/P** so duckweed grows without dosing → the self-sustaining loop (feed→manure→pond→feed) | set-and-top-up |
| **Small solar aerator / circulator** | prevents stagnation/anaerobic zones; set-and-forget (your solar+battery) | none |
| **Full duckweed cover** | free **algae + mosquito + evaporation** control | maintain by harvesting |
| **Skimmer / mesh harvester** | makes the one routine task fast | weekly-ish |
| **Depth + shade control** | summer heat buffer (see cooling) | seasonal |
| *(optional)* a few **native small fish** | eat mosquito larvae if cover lapses | minimal |

⚠️ **Do NOT add EU-banned invasives** — **Azolla filiculoides, water hyacinth** (and likely water lettuce) are prohibited in the EU. Stick to **native Lemna**; check anything new against the **EU IAS list**. [I22](research/sources.md#i22)

**Why it's low-effort:** with cover + balanced nutrients + gentle aeration, a floating-plant pond largely self-regulates; you mostly just skim the harvest.

## Tropical / high-margin setup (warm season, no heating)
Pick warm-loving, high-value crops that **fit the May–Sept window** and **don't need winter heat**:
| Crop | Why it fits | Margin |
|------|-------------|--------|
| **Ginger & turmeric** ⭐ | plant spring → **harvest autumn → store/sell over winter** = perfect seasonal crop; love warmth + the pond's humidity | high |
| **Basil** (Thai/holy/sweet), **lemongrass** | warm-season herbs, fast, premium | high €/m² |
| **Specialty chillies** | thrive in summer heat | high |
| **Microgreens** | fast cash between crops | very high €/m² |
| **Cape gooseberry / exotic melons** | warm-season novelty | good |
| *(optional)* **Saffron** | hardy autumn-flowering corm, summer-dormant — no heat needed; laborious harvest | very high €/kg |
- **Skip vanilla / cardamom** (need year-round warmth → not seasonal).
- **Pond synergy:** harvested **duckweed → mulch / liquid feed** for the tropicals; the pond adds **humidity** ginger/turmeric love; the warm greenhouse is the duckweed's home too.

## Summer cooling (Suceava gets hot — yes, but cheap)
Duckweed and most crops suffer >~35°C, and a closed greenhouse on a 35–40°C day overheats. Cheap, mostly passive:
- **Big ventilation** (auto roof + side vents / roll-up sides) — main tool.
- **Retractable shade cloth 30–50%**.
- **Run pond water deeper in summer (30–40 cm)** to buffer heat (15–20 cm in spring for fast warming). [I18](research/sources.md#i18)
- **Evaporative cooling** (wet-pad + fan / fogging) for heatwaves — modest cost, runs off your solar+battery.
- ~**€3–8k** for the small unit.

## Indicative budget (seasonal, ~150 m², own land, free engineer)
~**€45–70k** all-in (no boiler, no winter lighting): insulated polytunnel/twin-wall + shade/vents + shallow basins + small solar & second-hand batteries (pumps/aeration/frost) + drip + pond gear. **AFIR DR-14 (€75k, 85%)** can cover most — mind the **reimburse-after-spend** bridge. [I9](research/sources.md#i9)

## Annual rhythm (low-effort, solo)
- **Apr–May:** start pond, plant ginger/turmeric/herbs.
- **Jun–Aug:** harvest duckweed weekly (feed/fertiliser), sell herbs/microgreens, manage cooling.
- **Sep–Oct:** harvest ginger/turmeric, dry surplus duckweed for winter feed.
- **Nov–Mar:** sell stored produce; greenhouse dormant (no heating cost).
