## Workflow
1. Fill the **brief**, then the **hard questions** — gaps become research tasks.
2. Use the [`knowledge/`](../../knowledge/) base for the Romanian context (formation, tax, funding, compliance, institutions).
3. Build the **market analysis** and **financial model**; pick the tax regime explicitly.
4. Walk the **compliance checklist** for the chosen legal form and sector.
5. Maintain the **risk register** and **decision log** as you learn.
6. Score with the [evaluation rubric](../../framework/evaluation-rubric.md); record the score in the brief.
