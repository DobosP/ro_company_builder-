- [ ] **AFIR DR-23** processing grant application (€164.9M call, ~Dec 2025) [A5](research/sources.md#a5)
- [ ] **AFIR irrigation** equipment subsidy (up to ~$540k/farm) [A2](research/sources.md#a2)

## Food safety / processing
- [ ] **ANSVSA** sanitary-veterinary **registration/authorization** (Order 57/2010): dossier → county DSVSA on-site evaluation → unique number, before operating [A10](research/sources.md#a10)
- [ ] HACCP plan; cold-chain & traceability
- [ ] Environmental permit / EIA screening for the processing unit
