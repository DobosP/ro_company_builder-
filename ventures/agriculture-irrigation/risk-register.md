## Single biggest assumption
We can secure **reliable irrigation water on fertile land**, and **crop + processing margins clear the hurdle** once grants and APIA are netted out.

## Kill criteria
- [ ] No site with dependable water (channel + permit) → stop or pivot to processing-only (contracted supply).
- [ ] No-grant case far below hurdle **and** AFIR grant not won.
- [ ] No offtake interest for the chosen processed product.
