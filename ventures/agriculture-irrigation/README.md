## Snapshot
- **What:** integrated **irrigated vegetable production + processing** (e.g. tomato paste / frozen-canned veg), substituting imports.
- **Why now:** Romania imports **>80%** of its fruit & veg [A7](research/sources.md#a7); a **€15.83B** CAP plan + **€164.9M** AFIR processing call (Dec 2025) + irrigation subsidies make capital cheap. [A3](research/sources.md#a3) [A5](research/sources.md#a5)
- **Hard truths:** thin, weather-exposed margins; **irrigation + water rights** are the binding enabler (>75% of infra is dead [A1](research/sources.md#a1)); operationally heavy (farming *and* food processing).
- **Stage:** ☑ Idea ☑ Validate (desk) ☐ Model ☐ Form ☐ Operate — see [lifecycle](../../framework/venture-lifecycle.md)
