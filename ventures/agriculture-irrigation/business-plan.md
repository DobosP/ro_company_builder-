# Business Plan — Agriculture / Irrigation (Romania)

> Draft v1, verified 2026-05. Pairs with [financial-model.md](financial-model.md) and [hard-questions.md](hard-questions.md). [A#] → [sources](research/sources.md).

## 1. Executive summary
Build an **integrated irrigated production + processing** venture that substitutes imported fruit & veg products (e.g. tomato paste, frozen/canned veg). Phase 1: a **processing + cold-storage unit** (AFIR DR-23 grant) sourcing contracted local crops; Phase 2: add **~200 ha of own irrigated production** (AFIR irrigation subsidy + APIA payments).

## 2. Problem & solution
Romania imports >80% of fruit & veg despite vast farmland; near-dead irrigation and no value-add are the causes. We grow + process locally what is imported. [A1](research/sources.md#a1) [A7](research/sources.md#a7)

## 3. Market & competition
See [market-analysis.md](market-analysis.md): a multi-billion-€ import gap; compete on freshness, local sourcing, scale and grant-funded efficiency. [A6](research/sources.md#a6)

## 4. Product
A focused processed line (start with one: tomato paste **or** frozen/canned veg) + fresh sales of surplus; expand the range once the chain works.

## 5. Go-to-market
Offtake agreements / LOIs with a **retailer or food manufacturer**; private-label or own brand; cold-chain logistics.

## 6. Operations
Two competencies — **farming** (agronomy, irrigation, seasonal labour) and **food processing** (HACCP, cold chain, ANSVSA). Phase 1 contracts local growers to de-risk the farming build-out. [A10](research/sources.md#a10)

## 7. Legal & compliance
SRL; APIA/IACS registration; **Apele Române** water permit; **ANSVSA** food authorization; AFIR grant compliance; optional **OUAI** membership. See [compliance-checklist.md](compliance-checklist.md). [A9](research/sources.md#a9) [A10](research/sources.md#a10)

## 8. Financials
Illustrative integrated case (200 ha + processing): see [financial-model.md](financial-model.md) / [finsim.json](finsim.json). Thin but grant-supported; APIA payments add a stable floor. [A4](research/sources.md#a4)

## 9. Funding plan
Equity + project debt + **AFIR DR-23 processing grant** (€164.9M call, Dec 2025) + **irrigation subsidy** (up to ~$540k/farm) + ongoing **APIA** payments. PNRR irrigation money was cut. [A5](research/sources.md#a5) [A2](research/sources.md#a2) [A12](research/sources.md#a12)

## 10. Milestones & risks
See §11 of [hard-questions.md](hard-questions.md) and [risk-register.md](risk-register.md).

## 11. Impact
Food security, import substitution, rural jobs, water-efficient irrigation — aligned with CAP eco-schemes that part-fund it. [A4](research/sources.md#a4)
