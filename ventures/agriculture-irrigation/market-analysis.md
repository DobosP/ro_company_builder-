# Market Analysis — Agriculture / Irrigation (Romania)

> Verified 2026-05. [A#] → [sources](research/sources.md). Not investment advice.

## The gap (import substitution)
Romania imports **>80%** of its fruit & veg and most processed food; it imported ~5× more fruit/veg than it exported (~€2B in five months), and buys tomato paste from China. [A7](research/sources.md#a7) The food trade deficit is ~**€3.65B** within a €29.77B total. [A6](research/sources.md#a6) Biggest deficit lines: **meat, dairy, sweets/snacks, canned fruit & veg**. [A6](research/sources.md#a6)

## Market sizing
| Layer | Definition | Estimate | Basis |
|-------|-----------|----------|-------|
| **TAM** | RO fruit/veg + processed-food imports | **billions €/yr** | [A6](research/sources.md#a6) [A7](research/sources.md#a7) |
| **SAM** | one product category we can supply (e.g. tomato paste / frozen veg) | tens–hundreds €M | judgment |
| **SOM** | our realistic share, 1 processing unit + ~200 ha | low €M revenue | judgment |

## Customer / who pays
- **Retailers & food manufacturers** seeking local, traceable supply (import substitution).
- **APIA** per-ha direct payments (~€250/ha) underpin the farming layer. [A4](research/sources.md#a4)

## Competition & substitutes
| Competitor | Strength | Weakness | Our edge |
|-----------|----------|----------|----------|
| EU importers (DE/HU/PL/NL) | scale, reliability | freight, freshness, FX | local, fresh, shorter chain |
| Fragmented RO smallholders | low cost, local | sub-scale, no processing/cold chain | scale + processing + grants |
| Big domestic processors | brand, distribution | limited local raw supply | integrated own/contracted supply |

## Demand evidence
Persistent, large import dependency; rising drought (favours irrigated supply); strong policy push + funding (CAP €15.83B, AFIR DR-23 €164.9M, irrigation subsidies). [A3](research/sources.md#a3) [A5](research/sources.md#a5)

## Geography
**Class I–III irrigable land** near a **functioning ANIF channel** with reliable **Apele Române** abstraction — southern/SE plains (Brăila, Ialomița, Olt, Dolj). High-fertility siting is the **opposite** of solar. [A1](research/sources.md#a1) [A9](research/sources.md#a9)
