# Hard Questions — Agriculture / Irrigation (Romania)

> Answers to [`../../framework/hard-questions.md`](../../framework/hard-questions.md). Evidence in [research notes](research/research-notes.md); [A#] → [sources](research/sources.md). Verified 2026-05.

## 1. Problem & need
Romania imports **>80%** of fruit & veg and most processed food, running a structural food trade deficit (~€3.65B of a €29.77B total) despite being an arable giant. [A6](research/sources.md#a6) [A7](research/sources.md#a7) The cause: raw-commodity exports, near-dead irrigation (>75% non-functional), and little value-add. [A1](research/sources.md#a1) **Why now:** a €15.83B CAP plan + AFIR processing call + irrigation subsidies + drought pressure. [A3](research/sources.md#a3) [A5](research/sources.md#a5)

## 2. Market & demand
- **Who pays:** retailers & food manufacturers (import substitution); plus **APIA** per-ha payments. [A4](research/sources.md#a4)
- **Size:** ~€2B+ fruit/veg imports; tomato paste, canned/frozen veg, dairy, meat are the biggest deficit lines. [A7](research/sources.md#a7) [A6](research/sources.md#a6)
- **Competition:** fragmented small domestic producers + strong EU importers (DE, HU, PL, NL); win on freshness, local sourcing, scale and grant-funded efficiency. See [market-analysis.md](market-analysis.md).

## 3. Legal structure
- **SRL** (CIT at processing scale; micro 1% only if turnover ≤ €100k — unlikely here). [taxation](../../knowledge/taxation.md)
- Consider an **OUAI** membership for state-channel irrigation water; possibly a cooperative for sourcing.
- **CAEN:** crop growing (011x), processing/preserving fruit & veg (e.g. **1039**), support activities (016x) — *[TODO: verify Rev. 3 codes]*.

## 4. Regulation, permits & licensing
- **APIA** — IACS/LPIS land registration + annual single application (direct payments). [A4](research/sources.md#a4)
- **AFIR** — investment-grant applications (DR-23 processing, DR-12, irrigation). [A5](research/sources.md#a5)
- **Apele Române** — water-abstraction permit, **before works**. [A9](research/sources.md#a9)
- **ANSVSA** — food-unit sanitary-veterinary authorization (Order 57/2010; county DSVSA evaluation). [A10](research/sources.md#a10)
- **ANIF / OUAI** — access to state irrigation channels. See [compliance-checklist.md](compliance-checklist.md) and [institutions.md](../../knowledge/institutions.md).

## 5. Capital & funding
- Integrated CAPEX (illustrative): irrigation (~€3k/ha), processing line + cold storage, land/lease. [A8](research/sources.md#a8)
- **Grants:** AFIR **DR-23 processing €164.9M** (launch Dec 2025); **irrigation** up to ~$540k/farm; **APIA** ~€250/ha/yr. [A5](research/sources.md#a5) [A2](research/sources.md#a2) [A4](research/sources.md#a4)
- **PNRR irrigation was cut (~€3B)** → rely on CAP + state budget. [A12](research/sources.md#a12)
- Mix: equity + project debt + AFIR grant + ongoing APIA payments.

## 6. Financial viability
- **Revenue:** processed-product sales (the margin) + fresh sales + APIA payments.
- **Margins are thin and weather-exposed** for raw crops; **processing + irrigation** lift and stabilise them. Tax: CIT 16%; VAT — note **food is in the 11% reduced band**. [taxation](../../knowledge/taxation.md)
- Run it: see [financial-model.md](financial-model.md) / [finsim.json](finsim.json). Sensitivity: yield (drought), output prices, grant intensity, energy/water cost.

## 7. Team & operations
- Needs **two competencies**: farming (agronomy, irrigation) **and** food processing (HACCP, ANSVSA, cold chain). Likely hire/partner for the gap; contract local growers in phase 1.
- Seasonal labour; energy and water are major opex lines.

## 8. Location & strategic fit
- **Class I–III irrigable land** near a **functioning ANIF channel** with a reliable **Apele Române** abstraction (south/SE plains: Brăila, Ialomița, Olt, Dolj). [A1](research/sources.md#a1) [A9](research/sources.md#a9)
- Proximity to the channel and to logistics/retail matters; note: high-fertility-land siting is the **opposite** of the solar rule.

## 9. Risks & mitigations
Top risks (full table in [risk-register.md](risk-register.md)): **drought / water-supply failure**; **price volatility**; **grant timing/intensity**; **execution** (farming + processing); **input/energy cost**. Mitigate with reliable water rights, contracts/offtake, phasing, and irrigation efficiency.

## 10. Impact & theory of change
Food security, **import substitution**, rural jobs, drought resilience, and water-efficient irrigation — strong ESG/CAP-eco alignment (eco-schemes pay for it). [A4](research/sources.md#a4)

## 11. Timeline & milestones
1. Land + water + ANIF/OUAI shortlist — *months 0–4*.
2. Product + offtake LOIs; **DR-23 processing** application — *track Dec 2025 call*. [A5](research/sources.md#a5)
3. Apele Române permit + ANSVSA pre-assessment — *months 3–9*. [A9](research/sources.md#a9) [A10](research/sources.md#a10)
4. AFIR grant + financing close; build processing + irrigation — *months 9–18*.
5. First processing season + APIA enrolment — *year 2*.
- **Smallest real step:** a processing unit sourcing contracted local crops (defer own-farm CAPEX).
