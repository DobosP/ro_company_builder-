## 2. Executive summary
A **vegetable processing + cold-storage unit** (e.g. tomato paste / frozen-canned veg) substituting imports, sourcing from contracted local growers. Eligible CAPEX **€4.0M**; **grant requested €2.6M** (**65%**, under the €3M cap); **co-financing €1.4M** (equity + debt).

## 3. Alignment with objectives & priorities
Hits DR-23's **priority sector** principle — **trade-balance + food security** — directly: Romania imports >80% of fruit & veg and tomato paste from China. [A7](research/sources.md#a7) [A6](research/sources.md#a6)
