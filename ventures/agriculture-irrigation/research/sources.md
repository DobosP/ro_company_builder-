# Sources — Agriculture / Irrigation venture

Venture-specific sources. Reusable Romania facts → [`../../../knowledge/sources.md`](../../../knowledge/sources.md). Re-verify before acting; CAP calls and rates move.

<a id="a1"></a>**A1.** Business Review — how Romania's irrigation was run down: ~3M ha system (85% Danube-fed), **>75% degraded / non-functional** — https://business-review.eu/business/agriculture/irrigation-system-202654 — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="a2"></a>**A2.** trade.gov — Romania irrigation infrastructure: 10,630 km channels, 26,700 km buried pipe, 2,700+ pumping stations; ANIF national rehab of ~2,004,639 ha; **$1.6B** state budget 2022–27; EU covers up to ~**$540k/farm** for new irrigation (AFIR) — https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/romania-irrigation-infrastructure-development — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="a3"></a>**A3.** EU CAP Network / oportunitati-ue — Romania **CAP Strategic Plan (PNS) 2023–27 = €15.83B** (Pillar I direct payments + Pillar II rural dev; ~61% subsidies, ~38% investments/eco) — https://oportunitati-ue.gov.ro/en/program/planul-national-strategic-pac-2023-2027/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="a4"></a>**A4.** APIA / AgroInfo — 2025 direct payments per ha: **BISS €99.27**, CRISS ~€50–52 (1–50 ha), CIS-YF ~€46 (young farmer), eco PD-28 ~€58; combined can exceed **€250/ha** — https://agroinfo.ro/cati-bani-primiti-pe-hectar-de-la-apia-din-16-octombrie-2025/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="a5"></a>**A5.** Agroberichten Buitenland — AFIR calls: **DR-23 Processing €164.9M** (launch ~15 Dec 2025); **DR-12 young farmers €169.5M** (~30 Jan 2026); 102 processing projects = €259.3M; ~€501M agri+processing early 2025 — https://www.agroberichtenbuitenland.nl/actueel/nieuws/2025/01/29/first-eu-funds-for-romania-this-year-food-processing-small-farmers-and-settled-young-farmers — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="a6"></a>**A6.** The Diplomat Bucharest — Romania trade deficit **€29.77B** (11mo 2025); food & live animals **~€3.65B**; structural raw-export / processed-import imbalance — https://www.thediplomat.ro/2026/02/04/romanias-trade-deficit-reaches-29-77-billion-euros-food-industry-a-major-contributor-penny-study-shows/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="a7"></a>**A7.** EastFruit / FreshPlaza — **>80% of fruit & veg consumption imported**; imported ~5× more fruit/veg than exported (~€2B in 5 months); tomato paste imported from China — https://east-fruit.com/en/news/romanian-vegetable-sector-struggles-with-rising-imports/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="a8"></a>**A8.** trade.gov / Farmonaut — irrigation CAPEX: drip ~$850–1,200/ha, centre-pivot ~$800–1,200/ha (full kit higher); O&M 3–5%/yr (drip), ~$300–600/ha/yr (pivot) — https://www.trade.gov/market-intelligence/romania-irrigation-infrastructure-development — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="a9"></a>**A9.** Apele Române (rowater.ro) — water-management permit/authorization required at design/feasibility, **before works**, for water abstraction — https://rowater.ro/institution-activity/departments/integrated-european-water-resources-management/permits-and-authorizations/?lang=en — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="a10"></a>**A10.** ANSVSA — food unit **sanitary-veterinary registration/authorization** (Order 57/2010); county DSVSA on-site evaluation → unique authorization number — https://www.ansvsa.ro/industrie-si-afaceri/inregistrare-si-autorizare/ — *verified 2026-05*

<a id="a11"></a>**A11.** Agroberichten Buitenland — Romania 2025 crop production: maize **1.97M ha** (EU #1 area); larger farms far more likely to irrigate — https://www.agroberichtenbuitenland.nl/actueel/nieuws/2026/04/15/romanias-2025-crop-production-in-figures — *verified 2026-05*
