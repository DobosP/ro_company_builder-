# Research Notes — Agriculture / Irrigation in Romania

> Evidence base for this venture. Verified **2026-05**; not investment/legal advice — CAP calls and rates change. Citations [A#] → [`sources.md`](sources.md); reusable Romania facts → [`../../../knowledge/`](../../../knowledge/).

## 1. The thesis: import substitution, enabled by irrigation
Romania is an arable giant (EU #1 in maize area, 1.97M ha) [A11](sources.md#a11) but **exports raw commodities and imports high-value processed food**. The 2025 trade deficit hit **€29.77B**, with food & live animals **~€3.65B** of it. [A6](sources.md#a6) **>80% of fruit & veg consumed is imported**; Romania imports ~5× more fruit/veg than it exports (~€2B in five months) and buys tomato paste from China despite ample land. [A7](sources.md#a7) The opportunity is to **produce and process locally** what is currently imported — and the binding enabler is **irrigation** (and capital), heavily de-risked by grants.

## 2. Irrigation — the missing infrastructure
- Romania's irrigation system spans ~**3M ha** (85% Danube-fed), but **>75% is degraded or non-functional**. [A1](sources.md#a1)
- Infrastructure: 10,630 km channels, 26,700 km buried pipe, 2,700+ pumping stations, managed by **ANIF** (national land-improvement agency). [A2](sources.md#a2)
- **ANIF** is rehabilitating ~**2,004,639 ha** (88 facilities); **$1.6B** state budget 2022–27 for primary canals/drainage. [A2](sources.md#a2)
- Farmers access secondary infrastructure via **OUAI** (water-users organizations; OUG 147/1999). Water abstraction needs an **Apele Române** permit *before* works. [A9](sources.md#a9)
- ⚠️ ~**€3B** of irrigation money was **dropped from the PNRR** → rely on CAP + state budget + AFIR equipment subsidies, not PNRR. [A12](sources.md#a12)

## 3. Funding — a deep, grant-rich stack
- **CAP Strategic Plan (PNS) 2023–27 = €15.83B** (Pillar I direct payments via APIA + Pillar II rural dev via AFIR). ~61% subsidies, ~38% investments/eco-schemes. [A3](sources.md#a3)
- **APIA direct payments (annual, per ha):** BISS €99.27, CRISS ~€50–52 (1–50 ha), CIS-YF ~€46 (young farmer), eco-scheme PD-28 ~€58 → **combined can exceed €250/ha**. [A4](sources.md#a4)
- **AFIR investment grants (the engine for this venture):**
  - **DR-23 Processing of agri products** — **€164.9M**, launch ~**15 Dec 2025** (milling, baking, canning, etc.); 102 processing projects = €259.3M. [A5](sources.md#a5)
  - **DR-12 young farmers (consolidation)** — €169.5M, ~30 Jan 2026 (settled young farmers <45). [A5](sources.md#a5)
  - **Irrigation equipment** partially compensated by AFIR; EU covers up to ~**$540k/farm**. [A2](sources.md#a2)
- Several calls open **Dec 2025–Feb 2026** (~€501M agri+processing). [A5](sources.md#a5)

## 4. Regulation & permits
- **APIA** — register land in IACS/LPIS; file the annual single application for direct payments. [A4](sources.md#a4)
- **AFIR** — investment-project applications under the DR interventions. [A5](sources.md#a5)
- **Apele Române (ANAR)** — water-management permit/authorization for abstraction, obtained at feasibility, **before works**. [A9](sources.md#a9)
- **ANSVSA** — food-processing units need **sanitary-veterinary registration/authorization** (Order 57/2010): county DSVSA on-site evaluation → unique authorization number, required before operating. [A10](sources.md#a10)
- **ANIF / OUAI** — for state-channel water, join/form a water-users organization.
- Land: APIA registration; for farming you want **high-fertility (Class I–III) land** (the opposite of the solar-siting rule). See [institutions.md](../../../knowledge/institutions.md).

## 5. Economics (orders of magnitude)
- **Irrigation CAPEX:** drip ~$850–1,200/ha; centre-pivot ~$800–1,200/ha (full kit higher); O&M 3–5%/yr (drip) or ~$300–600/ha/yr (pivot) — AFIR-subsidized. [A8](sources.md#a8)
- **Land:** ~**€8,000–9,000/ha** to buy (lease cheaper) — see [`../../../knowledge/open-questions.md`](../../../knowledge/open-questions.md) R14.
- **Margins:** rain-fed cereals are thin and drought-volatile; **irrigated high-value veg + processing** carry much higher, more stable margins (and capture the imported-product premium). Crop-specific gross margins are an open modelling task (see §7).
- See [`../financial-model.md`](../financial-model.md) for an illustrative integrated case.

## 6. Strategic options
- **A. Irrigated high-value production** (veg/fruit) — land + water + AFIR-subsidized irrigation.
- **B. Processing** (DR-23 grant) — tomato paste / frozen-canned veg / cold storage; clearest grant + clearest import gap.
- **C. Integrated produce + process** — captures full margin and the richest grant stack, but most capital + operational complexity.
- **D. Irrigation/OUAI services** — asset-light-ish but tied to state infrastructure.
→ Recommended: **C, phased** — start processing (DR-23, clear market gap) sourcing from contracted local farms, then add own irrigated production. See [decision-log.md](../decision-log.md).
