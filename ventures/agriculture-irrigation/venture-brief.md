## Why now
A €15.83B CAP plan + AFIR processing call (Dec 2025) + irrigation subsidies coincide with a wide, persistent import gap and worsening drought that rewards irrigated supply. [A3](research/sources.md#a3) [A5](research/sources.md#a5) [A7](research/sources.md#a7)

## Biggest risk / open question
**Water + land:** can we secure **Class I–III irrigable land with a reliable water permit** (Apele Române) and functioning ANIF/OUAI channel — and do the **crop+processing margins** clear the hurdle once grants are netted out? [A1](research/sources.md#a1) [A9](research/sources.md#a9)

## Next 3 actions
1. **Land + water scouting** — irrigable Class I–III plots near a live ANIF channel; check OUAI + Apele Române abstraction.
2. **Target the DR-23 processing call** — product choice (tomato paste vs frozen veg), grant intensity, deadline. [A5](research/sources.md#a5)
3. **Offtake** — letters of intent from a retailer/manufacturer for the chosen processed product.
