# Compliance Checklist — Energy Storage (Romania)

> BESS-specific, derived from [research notes](research/research-notes.md) and [`../../knowledge/`](../../knowledge/). Not legal advice — confirm with ANRE / a lawyer. [E#] → [sources](research/sources.md).

## Formation (ONRC)
- [ ] SRL (and/or per-site **SPV**) registered; CAEN incl. electricity production/trading — *[TODO: verify Rev. 3 storage code]* [E15](research/sources.md#e15)
- [ ] CUI + J-number — see [company-formation](../../knowledge/company-formation.md)

## Grid connection (the gating step — Order 20/2025)
- [ ] Connection request filed; **complete file** for prioritization [E14](research/sources.md#e14)
- [ ] **Solution study** received; **financial guarantee** posted within ~2 months (else lapses) [E14](research/sources.md#e14)
- [ ] **ATR** (*aviz tehnic de racordare*) obtained; note any **operational limitation** [E14](research/sources.md#e14)
- [ ] Guarantees budgeted: **€20k/MW** (capacity) + **€30k/MW** (establishment auth.) + **20% of connection tariff** [E13](research/sources.md#e13)

## ANRE authorizations
- [ ] **Establishment authorization** (*autorizație de înființare*) — required **> 1 MW** [E13](research/sources.md#e13)
- [ ] **Operating licence** before commercial operation (storage "assimilated to producers", Order 27/2025) [E15](research/sources.md#e15)

## Construction & environment
- [ ] Building permit (*autorizație de construire*) [E13](research/sources.md#e13)
- [ ] Environmental sign-off / EIA screening
- [ ] Land control (lease/option) + urbanism certificate (PUZ if needed)

## Market participation
- [ ] Registration to trade on **OPCOM** (DAM/IDM) and/or via a Balancing Responsible Party / optimiser [E12](research/sources.md#e12)
- [ ] Metering & telemetry to Transelectrica for balancing [E11](research/sources.md#e11)

## Fiscal & reporting (standard)
- [ ] ANAF registration; **CIT** regime; **VAT** registration (recover CAPEX VAT)
- [ ] e-Factura / SAF-T / (e-Transport if moving equipment) — see [regulatory-compliance](../../knowledge/regulatory-compliance.md)

## Funding compliance
- [ ] Modernisation Fund / CISAF state-aid eligibility met (entity registered with ONRC before first aid payment) [E2](research/sources.md#e2)
- [ ] If co-located: **≥75%** absorption-of-RES rule; **≤€10M**/enterprise [E3](research/sources.md#e3)
