## Snapshot
- **What:** develop/own/operate utility-scale standalone BESS, earning from balancing services + arbitrage, grant-supported by the Modernisation Fund.
- **Why now:** RES-driven congestion + negative prices; a **live €150M standalone scheme** (EC-approved Mar 2026, awards to 2030). [E2](research/sources.md#e2)
- **Hard truth:** capital-intensive and crowding fast; the scarce assets are **grid-connection rights + land**, not the batteries.
- **Stage:** ☑ Idea ☑ Validate (desk) ☐ Model ☐ Form ☐ Operate — see [lifecycle](../../framework/venture-lifecycle.md)
- **Rubric score:** see [decision-log.md](decision-log.md) (initial: **~84/115**)
