## Snapshot
- **What:** develop/own/operate a **hybrid** — 50 MW/100 MWh battery + ~10 MWp co-located solar — sharing one grid connection, earning from balancing + arbitrage + solar generation, grant-supported by the Modernisation Fund.
- **Why now:** RES-driven congestion + negative prices; a **live €150M standalone scheme** [E2](research/sources.md#e2); and a decisive market shift to **financed hybrid platforms**. [E24](research/sources.md#e24)
- **Hard truths:** capital-intensive and crowding fast; the scarce asset is the **grid-connection right + sited land**; and the **standalone vs co-located grant routes are mutually exclusive** for one battery. [E13](research/sources.md#e13) [E2](research/sources.md#e2) [E3](research/sources.md#e3)
- **Stage:** ☑ Idea ☑ Validate (desk) ☐ Model ☐ Form ☐ Operate — see [lifecycle](../../framework/venture-lifecycle.md)
- **Rubric score:** **88/115** (see [decision-log.md](decision-log.md))
