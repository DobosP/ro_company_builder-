# Hard Questions — Hybrid Solar-plus-Storage (Romania)

> Answers to [`../../framework/hard-questions.md`](../../framework/hard-questions.md). Evidence in [research notes](research/research-notes.md); [E#] → [sources](research/sources.md). Verified 2026-05. **v2 — hybrid** (battery + co-located solar).

## 1. Problem & need
Fast solar/wind growth outpaces grid flexibility → **congestion, curtailment, negative prices**, and rising merchant risk for pure solar. [E20](research/sources.md#e20) [E23](research/sources.md#e23) A hybrid stores cheap/negative-price energy and sells fast balancing + peak power. **Why now:** Modernisation Fund support + strong 2025 spreads + the capital shift to financed hybrids. [E2](research/sources.md#e2) [E12](research/sources.md#e12) [E24](research/sources.md#e24)

## 2. Market & demand
- **Who pays:** Transelectrica (balancing), OPCOM (arbitrage), a solar offtaker/PPA — via an optimiser. [E11](research/sources.md#e11)
- **Size:** ~1,200 MW storage target by 2030 vs ~241 MW live; solar adds a second revenue stream. [E1](research/sources.md#e1)
- **Competition:** crowded, now incl. hybrids (Gârla Mare 2 126 MWp + 200 MWh). [E24](research/sources.md#e24) [E7](research/sources.md#e7) [E8](research/sources.md#e8)
- **Edge:** grid-connection rights + land + speed + a financed hybrid (de-risked vs standalone).

## 3. Legal structure
- **SRL** per-site **SPV**; **CIT 16%** (micro irrelevant at scale). [taxation](../../knowledge/taxation.md)
- **CAEN Rev. 3:** **3512** (renewable production/solar) + **3516** (storage) + **3515** (trading). [E35](research/sources.md#e35)

## 4. Regulation, permits & licensing
- **Storage:** ANRE establishment authorization > 1 MW; ATR grid connection (Order 20/2025: operational limitation + guarantees). [E13](research/sources.md#e13) [E14](research/sources.md#e14)
- **Solar:** ANRE **setting-up authorization > 1 MW** (Order 6/2025; ≤1 MW exempt). [E26](research/sources.md#e26)
- **Land:** avoid Class I–III ag land (needs Ministry-of-Agriculture conversion); **<50 ha on Class III/IV/V extra-muros skips the PUZ**; MADR Order 4/2025 enables agrivoltaic grassland dual-use. [E27](research/sources.md#e27)
- Path: ATR → ANRE authorizations (storage + solar) → building permit → operating licence (+ environmental). See [compliance-checklist.md](compliance-checklist.md).

## 5. Capital & funding
- **Capital-heavy:** combined CAPEX ~**€30–42M** (battery ~€28M + 10 MWp solar ~€5.5M − shared-connection saving). [E16](research/sources.md#e16) [E29](research/sources.md#e29) [E28](research/sources.md#e28)
- **Grant (key choice):** battery via the **live standalone** scheme [E2](research/sources.md#e2), *or* **co-located behind-the-meter** (≤100% eligible cost, €10M cap, **≥75% solar-absorption** rule) [E3](research/sources.md#e3) — **mutually exclusive for one battery; verify.**
- **Mix:** equity + project debt + grant; hybrids attract capital more readily. [E24](research/sources.md#e24)

## 6. Financial viability
- **Revenue:** battery balancing 60–70% + arbitrage 20–30% (~€4–6M/yr) + solar ~€0.8–1.2M/yr (~13 GWh). [E11](research/sources.md#e11) [E12](research/sources.md#e12) [E22](research/sources.md#e22)
- **Tax/VAT:** CIT 16%; VAT 21% recoverable on CAPEX. [taxation](../../knowledge/taxation.md)
- **Sensitivity:** balancing-price decay + solar capture cannibalization; the battery partly offsets the latter (charge midday, sell peak). See [financial-model.md](financial-model.md). [E23](research/sources.md#e23)

## 7. Team & operations
Development (permitting/grid/land), hybrid EPC management, and **outsourced** trading/optimisation + O&M. [E9](research/sources.md#e9) Week-1 reality is development & paperwork.

## 8. Location & strategic fit
**The whole game:** a node with **grid headroom** + (for the hybrid) **high irradiation** + land. Co-location shares the connection and land, improving economics. [E13](research/sources.md#e13) [E22](research/sources.md#e22) [E28](research/sources.md#e28)
- **Candidate site: Suceava / Ilișești** — the reinforced **400/110 kV Suceava** node (Gădălin ring + Moldova interconnector), DSO **Delgaz Grid**, cheap ~1–2 ha extravilan on **DN17**. Bucovina's weak sun ⇒ go **standalone battery** here (not the hybrid). Full assessment: [site-suceava-ilisesti.md](site-suceava-ilisesti.md). [E42](research/sources.md#e42) [E43](research/sources.md#e43) [E47](research/sources.md#e47)

## 9. Risks & mitigations
Top risks (full table in [risk-register.md](risk-register.md)): grid connection denied/limited; balancing-price saturation; **solar capture cannibalization**; **grant-route misfit**; **solar land/agri permitting**; CAPEX/financing; crowding. Hybrid **reduces** overall merchant risk vs either asset alone. [E23](research/sources.md#e23)

## 10. Impact & theory of change
Real system benefit: more renewables generated **and** integrated, less curtailment, fewer negative-price hours, grid stability — usable for ESG-linked finance. [E20](research/sources.md#e20)

## 11. Timeline & milestones
1. Site + grid headroom + land shortlist (Class III–V) — *months 0–3*. [E27](research/sources.md#e27)
2. Land options + connection solution study + guarantees — *months 2–6*. [E13](research/sources.md#e13)
3. **Choose grant route** + bid when the call opens — *track 2026*. [E2](research/sources.md#e2) [E3](research/sources.md#e3)
4. ANRE authorizations (storage + solar) + building permit — *months 6–12*.
5. Hybrid EPC + financing close — *months 9–15*.
6. Build + energise — *months 12–24*.
7. Commercial operation + optimisation contract live.
- **Smallest real step:** one connection point with a valid solution study — a sellable asset on its own.
