## Why a partner matters here
The Suceava BESS needs three things this venture doesn't have in-house: **grid-connection / substation works** (the hard, ANRE/Delgaz-facing part), **SCADA / protection / metering** (for dispatch & compliance), and **EPC delivery**. A credible local partner also **de-risks the grant application** (graders want a deliverable project) and gives a **real CAPEX quote** to replace finsim's €280/kWh assumption.

## Recommendation
1. **Shortlist EnergoBit and Electrogrup** for **competitive budgetary EPC + grid-connection bids** on the Suceava 100 MWh battery. Both cover the substation/MV/SCADA scope at the Delgaz/Transelectrica node. The quotes replace the finsim CAPEX assumption and feed the Modernisation Fund [funding-application.md](funding-application.md).
2. **Name the chosen EPC in the grant application** — a credible Cluj delivery partner strengthens the bid and the financing case.
3. **Keep trading/optimisation separate** — use a market **optimiser/tolling** partner for the arbitrage+balancing stack (see [arbitrage-strategy.md](arbitrage-strategy.md)); Gravity/Heliotech is a *forecasting* add-on, not a balancing trader.
4. **EnergoBit = VINCI** now — pro: bankability and turnkey capability; con: pricing/leverage of a big group. Use the Electrogrup bid for competitive tension.
